## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rare tension between high-concept editorial rigor and tactile boutique playfulness. Built as an avant-garde nocturnal canvas, it draws direct inspiration from the bespoke creative director studio ethos—inverting the conventional gallery space into an immersive dark sanctuary punctuated by razor-sharp electric azure accents, tactile paper surfaces, and micro-geometric confetti details.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Neo-Brutalist precision**. It treats negative space as a physical element, pairing colossal display headlines with disciplined, monospaced metadata and pill-shaped interactive triggers. The intended emotional response is uncompromising authority, bespoke luxury, and dynamic creative pulse—tailored specifically for visionary founders, design-led studios, and boundary-pushing brands who reject commoditized interfaces.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep atmospheric graphites (`#141412` and `#1C1C1A`) that anchor the dark nocturnal canvas, paired against high-contrast off-white paper tones (`#FFFFF7`, `#F4F2EC`) for display typography and curated inversion modules.

- **Primary (`#328DE0`):** Electric Cerulean. Reserved for high-value interactive focal points, active state highlights, and brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#9EBEC5`):** Soft Dust Sky. Used for subdued section backdrops, transitional container fills, and architectural rhythm bands.
- **Tertiary (`#FEA0CB`):** Muted Orchid Pink. Deployed across whimsical micro-accents, editorial badges, and interactive feedback markers.
- **Micro-Confetti Accents (`#C3EB38`, `#FF6B35`, `#FEA0CB`, `#328DE0`):** Tiny 6px–8px floating or inline geometric squares that inject deliberate moments of boutique irreverence into structured grids.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the sculptural weight of **Syne** with the clinical precision of **JetBrains Mono**.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in lowercase or sentence-case Syne with tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`). Headlines must feel structural and monolithic, often sitting flush against column edges.
- **Numerals & Watermarks:** Large sequential numbers (e.g., service steps `01` to `06`) use Syne rendered in translucent graphite or soft primary washes, serving as architectural background markers.
- **Body & Metadata:** JetBrains Mono is utilized strictly for body copy, project categorization, metadata rows, and navigation links. It anchors the high-concept display titles with pragmatic, functional clarity. All metadata pairings follow uppercase formatting with loose letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column desktop grid collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile. 

- **Outer Enclosure:** A generous `margin` of `3rem` preserves canvas breathing room on wide viewports, stepping down to `1.25rem` on mobile.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** Sections are divided by dramatic macro-spacing (`space-3xl` / `6.5rem`) to create an editorial gallery cadence. Content groupings operate on strict multi-tier vertical spacing (`space-md` through `space-2xl`).
- **Showcase Grids:** Work previews and creative explorations operate in dual-column format with hairline gutters, featuring full-bleed imagery crowned by minimal monospaced bottom-bar captions.
- **Ticker / Marquee Runners:** Edge-to-edge full-bleed dynamic tracks with thin top and bottom hairline borders, rotating all-caps metadata bullets with pill dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally eliminates traditional drop shadows and multi-layered skeuomorphic blur stacks. Spatial depth is established through **architectural tonal layering** and **hairline contours**:

1. **Base Layer:** The primary void sits at `#141412`.
2. **Surface Containers:** Cards, showcase tiles, and process modules utilize `#1C1C1A` or inverted `#F4F2EC` canvases, delineated by a single `1px solid #33332E` hairline outline.
3. **Floating Micro-Overlays:** Subtle modal layers or tooltips receive a `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 247, 0.12)`) and background tint of `#262623` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
4. **Contrast Accents:** Visual weight is driven purely by stark luminosity differences—high-contrast typography and vivid micro-confetti puncturing deep muted surfaces.

## Shapes

The geometry balances **curved pill contours** against **taut architectural rectangles**:

- **Corner Radius Scale:** Standard container elements and preview card wrappers use a calibrated radius of `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`), rising to `1rem` on larger showcase modules and floating visual cards.
- **Interactive Pill Morphology:** All primary buttons, tag badges, and quick-filter chips adopt full pill geometry (`9999px` border-radius), creating distinctive tactile capsules that stand out against rectangular grid blocks.
- **Geometric Micro-Confetti:** Accents appear as perfectly unrounded, sharp `0px` squares (ranging from `6px` to `10px`), providing a sharp graphic juxtaposition to the soft pill badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Pill Action:** Pill-shaped outline with a `1px solid #FFFFF7` border or inverted solid fill. Padding is `0.625rem 1.5rem`. Typography is `body-md` in JetBrains Mono. Includes a trailing clean vector diagonal arrow (`↗`) for links and external targets. On hover, background shifts cleanly to `#328DE0` with text locking to `#FFFFF7`.
- **Secondary Capsule:** Borderless, translucent graphite background (`#262623`) with muted monochrome text (`#8A8A82`).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped hairline badges (`rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`). Displayed inline or grouped adjacent to primary headlines. Typography is `caption-caps` in JetBrains Mono, rendering services such as `WEB DESIGN`, `FRAMER DEV`, or `ART DIRECTION`.

### Project Showcase Cards
- Generous 2-column rectangular cards with subtle `0.5rem` corner radius and thin hairline border.
- Visual frame contains crisp mockups or typography specimens. Captions sit directly below the media on a single line: bold project name flush-left, category and production year flush-right in `body-sm` JetBrains Mono.

### Process & Step Cards
- Inverted or graphite-surfaced modular cards.
- Layout: Large serif/grotesque step identifier (`the story`, `the design`, `the launch`) highlighted with italicized editorial accents, followed by brief monospace synopsis copy and inline wireframe previews.

### Services Numbered List
- An expansive stacked row layout. Each entry features a giant watermark numeral (`numeral-watermark`) aligned beside a clean, massive `headline-lg` service title. Hovering highlights the active row with an electric cerulean (`#328DE0`) underline indicator.

### Input Fields
- Understated input lines or minimal rectangular blocks with `0.5rem` rounding. Hairline bottom or perimeter border (`#33332E`), focusing to crisp `#328DE0`. Monospaced placeholder text in `#8A8A82`.
